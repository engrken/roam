# ROAM tenant onboarding guide

**Product version:** 0.2.0  
**Last updated:** November 23, 2026

This guide is for rental-business Owners and Admins setting up a ROAM Workspace for the first time.

## 1. Complete your business profile
1. Open **Business settings**.
2. Confirm your business name, contact information, currency and timezone.
3. Use the correct pickup contact details so customers know how to reach your team.

## 2. Add branches and vehicles
1. Add your main pickup branch and its customer-facing address.
2. Register each rentable vehicle with its type, make, model, plate number, daily rate and security deposit.
3. Set each vehicle to **Available** only when it is ready to rent. Use Maintenance or Inactive when appropriate.
4. Review your fleet and public booking page on a mobile device.

## 3. Publish rental terms
1. Open **Rental terms**.
2. Explain driver eligibility, deposits, payment timing, cancellations, late returns, damage, fines and permitted use.
3. Save and check the terms on the public booking page. Customers must accept the current terms before sending a booking request.

## 4. Configure tenant-owned payment methods
1. Open **Rental payment methods**. This is available to Owners and Admins.
2. Configure the receiving details for GCash, Maya, QR Ph, bank transfer or cash.
3. Upload only genuine QR images issued for your business account. Check the account name and number carefully.
4. Keep a method disabled until its details have been checked. Enable only the methods your business accepts.
5. Review the customer payment page using a private booking portal.

Customers pay your business directly. ROAM does not receive or pool tenant rental funds. RoamPH subscription billing is separate. The current rental payment workflow is manual: customer submissions remain pending until your team verifies the money was actually received.

## 5. Test a booking and payment
1. Open the public booking page and submit a test booking with a test customer email.
2. Open **Bookings** and review the pending request. Confirm it only after checking vehicle availability and your rental requirements.
3. Open the private booking portal link.
4. Select an enabled payment method and submit a test payment claim only when you have a safe test payment arrangement.
5. Open **Payment verification** and compare the amount, reference and proof against your actual receiving account.
6. Approve only after funds are confirmed. An approved submission creates a verified rental payment ledger entry. Pending or rejected submissions do not count as collections.
7. Check **Financials** and confirm the balance and transaction history update correctly.

## 6. Use Financials and analytics
The Financials page distinguishes:
- **Total rent due:** value of confirmed, active and completed bookings.
- **Rent paid:** verified rental payments less recorded refunds.
- **Outstanding rent:** unpaid booking balances.
- **Overdue rent:** unpaid balances on active rentals whose scheduled return time has passed.
- **Transactions:** recorded payments and refunds.

A customer-uploaded proof is not a verified payment until an authorized team member approves it. Booking value is not profit. Consider expenses, service costs, deposits and refunds separately. Pro workspaces can use date-range and branch-filtered operational reports.

## 7. Make repeat bookings faster
At the public booking contact step, a returning customer can enter the same email address and mobile number used previously and select **Quick fill my details**. ROAM checks for a matching customer record within that tenant and fills in the saved name. The customer must review the details before continuing. The lookup does not expose previous bookings, IDs or private documents.

## 8. Invite your team safely
Give each team member an individual account and only the role needed for their work. Remove access when someone leaves. Never share OTPs, private booking links or customer identity documents. Follow your own privacy notice and applicable data-protection obligations.

## Before sharing your booking page publicly
- [ ] Business profile and timezone checked
- [ ] Branch address and pickup instructions checked
- [ ] Vehicle rates, deposits and availability checked
- [ ] Rental terms published
- [ ] Tenant-owned payment destinations checked and enabled as appropriate
- [ ] Test booking and private customer portal reviewed on mobile
- [ ] Payment verification and financial balances tested
- [ ] Team access reviewed
