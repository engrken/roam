## 2. Purpose, documents and order of priority

2.1 ROAM operates technology that may allow Hosts to publish vehicle listings, receive booking requests, communicate with prospective renters and manage rental transactions. Host appoints ROAM, on a non-exclusive basis, to provide those platform services for the listings Host submits and ROAM accepts.

2.2 This Agreement governs the relationship between ROAM and Host. It is **not** the vehicle rental contract with a Renter. Each confirmed rental is a separate contract between Host and the Renter identified in the booking confirmation, incorporating the **ROAM Renter Booking Terms** and the booking-specific information and conditions shown in the Booking Summary.

2.3 The documents applying to a transaction are: (a) mandatory law; (b) the Booking Summary and booking-specific conditions accepted before confirmation; (c) the Renter Booking Terms; (d) this Agreement; and (e) ROAM's general Terms & Conditions. A more specific term prevails only to the extent of a direct inconsistency and only for the relevant subject. No document overrides a non-waivable legal right or duty.

2.4 Host must not incorporate a separate rental form, waiver, fee schedule or policy after a booking is confirmed unless the Renter expressly accepts the change and the change is lawful. Host must provide ROAM with any additional Host rental conditions before publication so they can be reviewed and displayed before a Renter commits.

## 3. Definitions

**Booking** means a rental confirmed through the Platform. **Booking Summary** means the final pre-confirmation screen or record setting out the Host, vehicle, dates, pickup/return details, total price, payment recipient and method, deposit, cancellation terms, insurance information, usage restrictions and other material conditions. **Host Content** means listings, photographs, descriptions, rules, prices, documents and other material Host submits. **Platform Fees** means commissions, service fees or other amounts specifically disclosed to Host under Schedule 2. **Renter** means the person named as renter in a Booking. **Vehicle** means the motor vehicle identified in a listing or Booking.

## 4. Relationship of the parties

4.1 Host acts as an independent vehicle rental provider and principal. Host, not ROAM, decides whether to offer a Vehicle, sets its lawful rental terms and is responsible for delivering the rental service. Host is not an employee, partner, joint venturer or general agent of ROAM. ROAM has no authority to bind Host to a rental except to the limited extent expressly stated in the payment or booking flow.

4.2 Host must not represent that ROAM owns, leases, insures, maintains or operates the Vehicle, or that ROAM is the rental provider, unless the relevant transaction expressly identifies ROAM as such.

4.3 The legal character of the relationship is determined by the parties' actual conduct and applicable law. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to misclassify a relationship or contract out of any obligation imposed on ROAM or Host by law.

## 6. Host representations and warranties

Host represents, warrants and undertakes on an ongoing basis that:

6.1 Host is legally capable of entering this Agreement and has authority to offer the Vehicle for rental. Host is the registered owner or holds valid written authority from the owner, lessor, financing company and any other person whose consent is needed. Host has checked that any financing, lease, mortgage, fleet or insurance agreement permits the proposed rental activity.

6.2 The Vehicle is lawfully registered, has the registration classification and permissions appropriate to its actual use, and carries current compulsory insurance and any other cover required by law. Host shall maintain additional insurance reasonably appropriate to commercial or paid rental use, including confirmation from the insurer that the policy does not exclude the disclosed rental activity or approved renter-drivers. Host must disclose material coverage limits, exclusions, deductibles/excesses and claim conditions to ROAM and Renters before booking. ROAM does not prescribe or provide insurance and does not warrant that any policy will respond to a claim.

6.3 The Vehicle is roadworthy, safe, properly maintained, clean, accurately described and available for the confirmed Booking. Host shall inspect and maintain it in accordance with law, manufacturer requirements and prudent vehicle-rental practice; promptly address safety defects, recalls and maintenance needs; and never hand over a Vehicle known or reasonably suspected to be unsafe or legally prohibited from use.

6.4 The Vehicle has all legally required equipment, documents and safety items. Host shall provide the Renter with the documents and instructions legally required for lawful use, including current registration and insurance evidence as appropriate, emergency contacts and material operating instructions.

6.5 All listing details, photographs, prices, available dates, mileage limits, geographic limits, permitted uses, included accessories, transmission/fuel type, capacity, condition, damage history that must be disclosed, insurance information, restrictions and fees are accurate, complete, current and not misleading.

6.6 Host will comply with applicable laws and lawful government orders, including consumer protection, internet transactions, data privacy, business registration, tax, road traffic, vehicle registration, insurance, safety, anti-fraud and anti-discrimination requirements. Host shall obtain and maintain all permits, licences, approvals and tax registrations applicable to its actual activity.

6.7 Host will not offer a stolen, encumbered without authority, unsafe, recalled and unremedied, illegally modified, unregistered, uninsured or otherwise unlawful Vehicle; nor use the Platform to facilitate an unlawful transaction.

6.8 Host will provide the rental service with due care and skill, honor confirmed Bookings, respond promptly to Renters and complaints, and not engage in deceptive pricing, bait-and-switch conduct, abusive collection practices or unlawful discrimination.

## 7. Listings, availability and booking acceptance

7.1 Host grants ROAM a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence during the Term to host, reproduce, resize, format, translate where appropriate, display and distribute Host Content solely as necessary to operate, promote and support the listing and Platform. Host confirms it owns or is authorized to license Host Content and that its use does not infringe third-party rights.

7.2 Host must maintain accurate availability and block dates when the Vehicle is unavailable. Host shall not accept overlapping rentals or knowingly accept a Booking it cannot perform. A request is not a confirmed Booking until the Platform or Host sends the confirmation specified in the booking flow and any required payment condition is satisfied.

7.3 Host shall not request or accept off-platform payment, side agreements or a change to the price or material conditions that circumvents Platform rules or deprives the Renter of disclosed consumer protections. Any permitted direct-payment flow must be specifically enabled and identified by ROAM.

7.4 Host must ensure that the Listing and Booking Summary identify the person or business actually supplying the rental service. If the Host uses an affiliate, fleet company or third-party owner as actual provider, it must disclose that provider and obtain ROAM's approval before the listing is published.

## 8. Rental performance and handover

8.1 Host shall make the Vehicle available at the exact agreed place and time, in the condition represented, with the agreed fuel/charge level, accessories, documents and safety equipment. Host shall provide a contact who can resolve handover issues.

8.2 Before the Renter takes possession, Host and Renter shall complete a written or electronic condition report recording, at minimum, date/time, odometer, fuel/charge level, visible damage, warning lights, accessories, keys and photographs of all sides and material existing damage. Both parties should be given a copy. Host must not ask a Renter to sign a blank or materially incomplete report.

8.3 If the Vehicle is materially different from the Booking Summary, unsafe, unavailable, or missing a legally required document or item, Host shall not insist that the Renter accept it. Host shall promptly offer a lawful equivalent acceptable to the Renter or cancel and refund amounts due under the Renter Booking Terms and applicable law.

8.4 Host shall provide clear instructions for roadside assistance, emergency reporting, breakdowns, accidents, theft, claims, permitted drivers, permitted areas, mileage, fuel/charging, parking and return. Host must be reasonably reachable during the rental period.

8.5 At return, Host shall promptly inspect the Vehicle with the Renter where practicable, record odometer/fuel/charge level and new damage, take time-stamped photographs, and provide a copy of the return report. Host must not make a damage or deposit deduction without the evidence and process stated in the Renter Booking Terms.

## 9. Pricing, taxes, Platform Fees and settlement

9.1 All Host-set charges must be lawful, accurately calculated and disclosed before a Renter confirms. Host shall not impose an undisclosed surcharge, compulsory add-on or post-booking price increase. The Booking Summary must state the total price and clearly itemize rent, taxes, delivery/collection, mileage, cleaning, optional extras, Platform/Renter fees, deposit, insurance charges and any other amount payable.

9.2 Host shall pay ROAM the Platform Fees in Schedule 2. ROAM may deduct only fees and adjustments specifically authorized in that Schedule and disclosed to Host. No commission, reserve, penalty or chargeback allocation may be introduced retroactively for an already-confirmed Booking.

9.3 The parties shall use only the payment and settlement flow identified in Schedule 2. The flow must state who is the payee/merchant, who receives the funds, whether ROAM or a regulated third-party provider processes or transmits payments, when Host earns and receives funds, how refunds and chargebacks are handled, and whether any lawful reserve or hold applies. **Nothing in this Agreement describes ROAM as an escrow agent, trustee, bank or insurer.** No funds are held in escrow unless a separately reviewed and legally compliant arrangement expressly says so.

9.4 Host is responsible for its taxes, business registrations, tax invoices/receipts and accounting obligations arising from the rental activity and Host's proceeds. Host shall provide required tax documents to Renters and ROAM. ROAM is responsible for taxes legally imposed on ROAM's own fees and services.

9.5 ROAM may delay settlement of a genuinely disputed amount, suspected fraud, payment-provider reversal or legal hold while it investigates, but shall notify Host, review the matter promptly and release any undisputed balance when reasonably practicable. A hold must not be indefinite or used as an arbitrary penalty.

## 10. Cancellations, refunds, no-shows and changes

10.1 Before any listing is activated, Host shall complete Schedule 3 and select a cancellation policy that the Platform can display before booking. Each Booking must identify the applicable policy, relevant cutoff times, no-show definition, refund amount, fees retained (if any) and refund timing. No Booking may be confirmed with a blank, inaccessible or contradictory cancellation policy.

10.2 Host shall honor the policy accepted for a Booking. Host must not change it after confirmation without the Renter's informed agreement and subject to mandatory law.

10.3 If Host cancels, cannot provide the Vehicle as booked, or provides an unsafe or materially non-conforming Vehicle, Host shall promptly notify ROAM and the Renter, cooperate with an appropriate replacement or cancellation, and ensure the Renter receives the refund or other statutory remedy due. Host may not charge the Renter a cancellation fee for Host's failure to perform.

10.4 Host shall promptly process and fund refunds allocated to Host under the disclosed payment flow. Host shall cooperate with ROAM and payment providers on chargebacks, payment reversals and consumer complaints and shall not retaliate against a Renter for exercising a legal right.

## 11. Security deposits, damage and additional charges

11.1 Before confirmation, Host shall disclose the deposit amount, whether it is a card pre-authorization or collected money, the holder/payee, permitted deductions, evidence requirements and the date or maximum period for release or refund. Host must not describe a deposit as non-refundable unless a lawful, clearly disclosed charge applies.

11.2 Host may claim a reasonable and evidenced amount for loss or damage attributable to the Renter or an authorized driver, subject to the Renter Booking Terms, the insurance policy and applicable law. Host shall provide the handover/return reports, time-stamped photographs, repair estimate or invoice, calculation of any loss-of-use claim if legally recoverable, and an itemized explanation. Host shall allow a reasonable opportunity to dispute the claim before a non-urgent deposit deduction is finalized.

11.3 Host may charge tolls, parking, traffic penalties, fuel/charging shortfall, additional mileage, late return, cleaning or other usage-related charges only where the charge, calculation method and applicable rate were disclosed in the Booking Summary or arise under mandatory law. Host must provide supporting records and may not impose an arbitrary penalty or duplicate recovery. Any administrative fee must be disclosed, reasonable and legally permitted.

11.4 Host shall not charge the Renter for pre-existing damage, ordinary wear and tear, mechanical failure not caused by the Renter, or loss caused by Host's breach, negligence or failure to maintain/insure the Vehicle.

## 12. Accidents, theft, breakdown and roadside support

12.1 Host shall maintain and provide current emergency and insurer/assistance contact details. Host shall give Renters practical instructions for accidents, theft, breakdowns and claims, including required reporting deadlines and documents.

12.2 Host shall promptly cooperate with Renters, ROAM, authorities and insurers; preserve relevant evidence; arrange necessary repairs or lawful replacement transport where the Host's service or the Vehicle fails; and not instruct a Renter to conceal an incident, make a false statement or breach an insurance condition.

12.3 Host shall notify ROAM promptly of a serious accident, injury, theft, safety incident, police report, insurance claim, vehicle seizure, complaint or event that could affect a Booking or other users.

## 13. Complaints and cooperation

13.1 Host shall respond to a Renter complaint within [ONE BUSINESS DAY / APPROVED SERVICE LEVEL] and provide ROAM with relevant records and a proposed resolution promptly. Host shall cooperate with ROAM's internal redress process and any applicable DTI, NPC, LTO, law-enforcement, court or regulator inquiry.

13.2 The parties acknowledge that, where the Internet Transactions Act applies, its internal redress process is deemed exhausted if a complaint remains unresolved after seven (7) calendar days from filing. This does not remove any other statutory remedy or deadline.

13.3 ROAM may contact a Renter, review booking records, request supporting documents, facilitate communication, issue a platform-policy decision, or suspend a listing while a safety or legal concern is reviewed. ROAM's assistance does not make it the rental provider or waive duties imposed on it by law.

## 14. Host conduct and prohibited activity

Host shall not: (a) falsify identity, title, registration, insurance, vehicle condition or booking details; (b) list a Vehicle without lawful authority; (c) submit forged or altered documents; (d) evade Platform Fees or move a transaction off-platform contrary to the enabled flow; (e) discriminate unlawfully or harass a Renter; (f) collect, retain or use renter data for unrelated marketing or profiling without a lawful basis; (g) threaten, intimidate or unlawfully collect money from a Renter; (h) suppress or retaliate against complaints or truthful reviews; (i) offer a vehicle or service prohibited by law or identified by ROAM as unsafe; or (j) interfere with the Platform's security or operation.

## 15. Personal information and data protection

15.1 Each party shall comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its implementing rules and applicable National Privacy Commission issuances. Each party shall provide the privacy notices required for its own processing and process personal information only for specified, legitimate and proportionate purposes, with an applicable lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

15.2 Host will generally act as a personal information controller for renter data it decides to collect and use to assess eligibility, perform the rental, maintain records, manage deposits, handle incidents and comply with law. ROAM will generally act as a personal information controller for account administration, platform security, marketplace operations, payment/fraud prevention and its own compliance activities. If ROAM processes personal information solely on Host's documented instructions, the parties shall execute a separate data processing agreement before that processing begins. If they jointly determine purposes or independently disclose data, the parties shall document the appropriate data-sharing arrangement and notices.

15.3 A driver's licence is government-issued information and may be sensitive personal information under Philippine privacy law. Before collecting a licence image, licence number, government ID or other sensitive data, Host and ROAM must identify and document an applicable legal basis under the Act, provide a specific notice, obtain the required separate and informed consent where consent is the basis, and collect only what is necessary. A general acceptance of this Agreement is not a substitute for that specific notice or consent.

15.4 Host shall restrict access to renter data, use secure storage and transmission, prohibit unauthorized copying or onward disclosure, maintain a retention and deletion schedule, respond to privacy requests, and notify ROAM promptly of any suspected breach involving Platform data. Host shall not retain full ID/licence images longer than necessary or use them for marketing, credit scoring or unrelated purposes without a separate lawful basis and notice.

15.5 The ROAM Privacy Policy explains ROAM's processing. Host shall provide its own privacy notice and privacy contact details where Host is a controller. The parties shall cooperate with data subject requests, security incidents and regulatory inquiries in accordance with their respective legal roles.

## 16. Platform rights, moderation and suspension

16.1 ROAM may review, rank, hide, edit for technical formatting, suspend or remove a Listing or Host Content; request more evidence; restrict a feature; or suspend Host access where reasonably necessary for safety, law, user protection, suspected fraud, breach, non-payment or Platform integrity. Where practicable, ROAM will tell Host the reason and allow a response. Immediate action may be taken where delay creates a safety, fraud, legal or security risk.

16.2 ROAM may preserve records and disclose information to authorities where legally required. ROAM's verification and moderation measures do not replace Host's continuing obligations and do not guarantee a Listing, Vehicle, Host or Renter.

16.3 ROAM will operate the Platform with reasonable care, subject to maintenance, outages, third-party services and events outside its reasonable control. Nothing in this clause limits mandatory duties or liabilities imposed on ROAM by law, including duties applicable to digital platforms or e-marketplaces.

## 17. Indemnity and allocation of responsibility

17.1 To the extent permitted by law, Host shall indemnify and hold harmless ROAM, its operator and personnel against third-party claims, regulatory losses, damages, liabilities and reasonable external legal costs to the extent arising from: (a) Host's breach of this Agreement or law; (b) Host's ownership, possession, condition, maintenance, registration, insurance, listing or rental of a Vehicle; (c) injury, death, property damage, theft or loss caused by Host, the Vehicle or Host's failure to perform; (d) Host's taxes, employees, agents or subcontractors; (e) Host Content or documents supplied without rights or authority; or (f) Host's unlawful processing or disclosure of personal information.

17.2 Host is not required to indemnify ROAM to the extent a claim was caused by ROAM's own negligence, breach, fraud, wilful misconduct or statutory responsibility. ROAM shall give reasonably prompt notice of a claim, allow Host to participate in the defence, and not agree to a settlement imposing an admission or non-monetary obligation on Host without Host's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. ROAM may control the defence where its own interests or legal duties require it.

17.3 Each party remains responsible for its own acts and omissions. Nothing in this Agreement excludes or caps liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or capped, including liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, death or personal injury where prohibited, mandatory consumer or privacy obligations, or statutory platform duties.

## 18. Term, termination and effect on existing Bookings

18.1 This Agreement continues until terminated. Either party may terminate on [30] days' written notice. ROAM may suspend or terminate immediately where reasonably required for safety, fraud, serious breach, unlawful activity, loss of required authority or insurance, or compliance with law. Host may terminate immediately if ROAM materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy the breach within [15] days after written notice, where capable of remedy.

18.2 On termination, Host shall not accept new Bookings. Unless safety, law or the Renter requires otherwise, Host must fulfill existing confirmed Bookings or work with ROAM to cancel and refund Renters in accordance with the applicable booking terms and law. Termination does not erase accrued payment, refund, liability, privacy or dispute obligations.

18.3 Clauses intended by their nature to survive, including accrued payment, record-keeping, data protection, indemnity, liability, disputes and governing law, survive termination.

## 19. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect non-public business, security, pricing, personal and operational information received from the other, use it only for this Agreement, restrict access to personnel who need it and are bound by confidentiality, and disclose it only with authorization or as required by law. This obligation does not apply to information lawfully public or independently obtained without restriction.

## 20. Changes and notices

20.1 ROAM may update this Agreement for future activity by giving Host reasonable notice and an effective date. A material change to fees, payout mechanics, risk allocation or a confirmed Booking will not apply retroactively; Host's express acceptance may be required before continued participation.

20.2 Notices shall be sent to the email and address stated above or to an updated contact provided in writing. A notice is effective when delivered without an error notice, or when physically received, as appropriate.

## 21. Electronic acceptance and records

The parties may sign this Agreement electronically. ROAM shall retain a complete, versioned copy of the Agreement, the identity/authentication method used, the Host's affirmative acceptance, and the date/time and relevant audit information. Host may download or request a copy. Electronic acceptance is intended to comply with applicable electronic-commerce law; any mandatory formality remains applicable.

## 22. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. The parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve a dispute through the notice contacts above. If unresolved, either party may pursue remedies before a court or competent authority with jurisdiction under applicable law. No clause requires a Host or Renter to waive a non-waivable remedy, regulator complaint or statutory right.

## 23. General provisions

If a provision is invalid, it shall be narrowed only to the extent legally permissible and the remainder shall continue. A waiver must be written and applies only to the specific instance. Host may not assign this Agreement or transfer a Listing without ROAM's written approval. ROAM may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or successor that assumes its obligations, subject to law and notice. This Agreement and its completed Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties concerning Host's Platform participation.
